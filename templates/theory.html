RKF45 Theory

Initial Value Problem

$$ \frac{dy}{dx}=f(x,y) $$

The Runge–Kutta–Fehlberg Method is an adaptive numerical method used to solve ordinary differential equations.

Error Formula

$$ E=|y_{n+1}^{(5)}-y_{n+1}^{(4)}| $$

Step Size Formula

$$ h_{new}=0.84h\left(\frac{Tolerance}{E}\right)^{1/4} $$

Advantages

  • High accuracy
  • Automatic error control
  • Adaptive step size
  • Efficient computation
