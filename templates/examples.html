Example 1

Solve the initial value problem:

$$ \frac{dy}{dx}=x+y $$ $$ y(0)=1 $$ with step size: $$ h=0.1 $$

Step 1: Compute \(k_1\)

$$ k_1=h f(x_n,y_n) $$ $$ k_1=0.1(0+1) $$ $$ k_1=0.1 $$

Step 2: Compute \(k_2\)

$$ k_2=h f\left(x+\frac{h}{4},y+\frac{k_1}{4}\right) $$ $$ k_2=0.1[(0+0.025)+(1+0.025)] $$ $$ k_2=0.105 $$

Step 3: Compute \(k_3\)

$$ k_3=0.1(0.0375+1.03906) $$ $$ k_3=0.10766 $$

Step 4: Compute \(k_4\)

$$ k_4=0.1(0.0923+1.0965) $$ $$ k_4=0.11888 $$

Step 5: Compute \(k_5\)

$$ k_5=0.1(0.1+1.1101) $$ $$ k_5=0.12101 $$

Step 6: Compute \(k_6\)

$$ k_6=0.1(0.05+1.0532) $$ $$ k_6=0.11032 $$

Step 7: Compute 4th Order Approximation

$$ y_{n+1}^{(4)} = y_n+\frac{25k_1}{216} +\frac{1408k_3}{2565} +\frac{2197k_4}{4104} -\frac{k_5}{5} $$ $$ y_{n+1}^{(4)}=1.11034 $$

Step 8: Compute 5th Order Approximation

$$ y_{n+1}^{(5)} = y_n+\frac{16k_1}{135} +\frac{6656k_3}{12825} +\frac{28561k_4}{56430} -\frac{9k_5}{50} +\frac{2k_6}{55} $$ $$ y_{n+1}^{(5)}=1.11036 $$

Step 9: Error Estimate

$$ E= |y_{n+1}^{(5)}-y_{n+1}^{(4)}| $$ $$ E=|1.11036-1.11034| $$ $$ E=0.00002 $$

Example 2

$$ \frac{dy}{dx}=y-x^2+1 $$ $$ y(0)=0.5 $$

Step 1: Compute \(k_1\)

$$ k_1=0.1(0.5-0^2+1) $$ $$ k_1=0.15 $$

Step 2: Compute \(k_2\)

$$ k_2=0.1(0.5375-0.025^2+1) $$ $$ k_2=0.15369 $$

Step 3: Compute \(k_3\)

$$ k_3=0.15608 $$

Step 4: Compute \(k_4\)

$$ k_4=0.16574 $$

Step 5: Compute Final RKF45 Solution

$$ y_{n+1}^{(5)}=0.65741 $$

Step 6: Error Estimate

$$ E=0.00003 $$

Final Answer

$$ y(0.1)\approx0.65741 $$
